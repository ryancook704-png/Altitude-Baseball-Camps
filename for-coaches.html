What the partnership looks like

We own and run

  • Your camp's name and logo
  • The website and registration page
  • The family list and the playbook
  • Payments, insurance, and safety standards

You do

  • Coach the camp
  • Book the field
  • Hire and train your assistants
  • Bring in the families in your town
You commit to
A set number of camp hours each summer, and no competing baseball camps in your region while you are with us. We agree the details for your town together.
How we are paid
A share of your registration revenue, with no minimum payment. You pay local costs, such as the field and your staff. We go over the numbers on a call.
After three years
Renew on lower terms, or buy your camp from us.

This page describes a possible partnership and is not an offer to sell a franchise or business opportunity. Any offer is made only through the required written disclosures and an agreement, in states where it is permitted.
