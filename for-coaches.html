What the partnership looks like

What we own and run

Your camp's name and logo, its website and registration page, the family list, and the playbook. We collect registrations and pay you after camp, and we carry the insurance and the safety standards.

What you do

Coach the camp, book the field, hire and train your assistants, and bring the families in your town. You pay local costs, such as the field and your staff.

What you commit to

A set number of camp hours each summer, and no competing baseball camps in your region while you are with us. We agree the details for your town together.

How we are paid

A share of your registration revenue, with no minimum payment. We will go over the numbers with you on a call.

This page describes a possible partnership and is not an offer to sell a franchise or business opportunity. Any offer is made only through the required written disclosures and an agreement, in states where it is permitted.
