Questions coaches ask

The short answers. We go through the details on a call.

What does it cost to start?

There is no minimum payment. We earn a share of your registration revenue, and you pay your local costs, such as the field and your staff. We go over the numbers with you on a call.

How much of my time does it take?

The camp itself runs for a few weeks in the summer. Launch work happens over about six months, with one 30-minute check-in a week.

Can I keep my coaching or teaching job?

Most of the work is in the summer. We will also check with you for any school or district rules that apply to running a camp.

What if the camp does not fill?

You do not owe us a minimum. About eight weeks out we review sign-ups with you and adjust the dates or the marketing.

Who owns the camp?

We own the camp's name, website, and family list, and you run it. After three years you can renew on lower terms or buy the camp from us.

What do you ask of me?

A set number of camp hours each summer, and no competing baseball camps in your region while you are with us. We agree the details for your town together.
