The deal in plain terms

The answers coaches ask for first. We go through every detail on a call before anything is signed.

What it costs
There is no minimum payment. We earn a share of your registration revenue, and you cover local costs such as the field and your staff.
What you commit to
A set number of camp hours each summer, and your camp is the only baseball camp you run in your region while you are with us. We set the details for your town together.
Who owns the camp
We own the camp's name, website and family list, and you run it. After three years you can renew on lower terms or buy the camp from us.
Support along the way
About eight weeks out we review sign-ups with you and adjust the dates or the marketing, so the camp is set up to fill.
Your time
Launch work runs about six months with one 30-minute check-in a week, and the camp itself takes a few weeks in summer. We also check any school or district rules that apply to you.

Who this is for

We are looking for coaches with a following in their town and the drive to build a real business. It is not for a side gig with no plan to grow.
